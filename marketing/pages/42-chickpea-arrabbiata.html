Chickpea arrabbiata

Angry only in the sense that it has chilli in it. Twenty minutes, one pan, and nothing you do not already have.

Serves 2  ·  20 minutes
Ingredients
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, for frying
  • 1 large onion, finely sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 x 400g tin chopped tomatoes
  • 1 x 400g tin chickpeas, drained
  • 1 tsp caster sugar
  • Salt and black pepper
  • A handful of flat-leaf parsley, chopped
Method
  1. Warm the oil and cook the onion gently for 8 minutes, until soft and sweet.
  2. Add the garlic and chilli flakes and stir for a minute, until you can smell them.
  3. Tip in the tomatoes and the sugar. Simmer hard for 10 minutes, until the sauce darkens and thickens.
  4. Stir through the chickpeas, warm them, and season well. Finish with the parsley.
Good over pasta, better on toast with too much oil.
