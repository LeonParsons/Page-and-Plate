Butter bean and kale soup

The soup to make when the fridge is empty and the cupboard is not. It thickens as it stands.

Serves 4  ·  30 minutes
Ingredients
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, for frying
  • 1 large onion, finely sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1½ tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 x 400g tins butter beans, drained
  • 200g kale, shredded
  • 1 litre vegetable stock
  • Salt and black pepper
Method
  1. Soften the onion in the oil over a low heat for 10 minutes. Add the garlic and paprika for the last minute.
  2. Add the beans and the stock, bring to a simmer and cook for 10 minutes.
  3. Crush about a third of the beans against the side of the pan — that is what thickens it.
  4. Stir in the kale and cook for 4 minutes more, until just tender. Season generously.
A spoonful of pesto stirred in at the table does no harm.
