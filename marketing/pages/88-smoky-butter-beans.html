Beans & Pulses 88

Smoky butter beans with roasted peppers

A braise that asks almost nothing of you. Everything comes from the cupboard, and it is better on the second day than the first.

Serves 4  ·  35 minutes
Ingredients
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, for frying
  • 1 large onion, finely sliced
  • 3–4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1½ tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 x 400g tin chopped tomatoes
  • 2 x 400g tins butter beans, drained
  • 150g roasted red peppers, sliced
  • 1 tsp caster sugar
  • 100ml vegetable stock
  • Salt and black pepper
  • A handful of flat-leaf parsley, chopped
  • 200g/7oz feta, to crumble over
Method
  1. Warm the oil in a wide pan over a medium heat. Add the onion with a good pinch of salt and cook gently for 10 minutes, until soft and just beginning to colour at the edges.
  2. Stir in the garlic and smoked paprika and give it 30 seconds, no more — paprika turns bitter the moment it catches.
  3. Tip in the tomatoes, the sugar and the stock. Simmer for 12 minutes, until the sauce has thickened enough to hold the mark of a spoon.
  4. Fold through the butter beans and the peppers and warm everything for 5 minutes. Taste, and be generous with the pepper.
  5. Scatter with parsley and crumble the feta over the top. Eat with bread you do not intend to account for.
Swap the feta for a spoonful of yoghurt to keep it lighter.
